MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is made effective as of January 15, 2025, by and between TechProvider Solutions LLC ("Provider") and Client Global Operations Inc. ("Client").

RECITALS
WHEREAS, Provider specializes in software development and IT consulting services; and
WHEREAS, Client desires to retain Provider to perform professional services as set forth in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW");

NOW, THEREFORE, the parties agree as follows:

1. SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES
Provider shall perform the services described in each SOW executed by both parties. All deliverables shall conform to the specifications set forth in the applicable SOW.

2. ASSIGNMENT AND DELEGATION
Neither Party shall assign this Agreement or any of its rights or obligations hereunder, voluntarily or involuntarily, by operation of law or otherwise, without the express prior written consent of the other Party.

3. CAP ON LIABILITY
TO THE MAXIMUM EXTENT PERMITTED BY APPLICABLE LAW, IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY'S TOTAL CUMULATIVE LIABILITY FOR ALL CLAIMS ARISING OUT OF OR RELATING TO THIS AGREEMENT OR THE SERVICES PROVIDED HEREUNDER EXCEED THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CLIENT TO PROVIDER IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.

4. TERMINATION FOR CONVENIENCE
Client may terminate this Agreement or any SOW hereunder for convenience and without penalty, in whole or in part, at any time by giving Provider at least fifteen (15) calendar days prior written notice.

5. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without giving effect to any choice-of-law principles.
